# Issue brief template

Section 9 of the pack: for each pilot issue, prepare a one-page issue brief with a verified owner,
relevant procedure, evidence, counterargument, proposed change and safe challenge route. Invite a
small set of relevant groups to correct it. Record agreement, dissent and scope; obtain consent
before publishing names.

Copy the template below. One page. Where the pack is silent or you do not know, write "to be
confirmed" rather than guess. Indicative owners on the issue cards are not verified owners until a
human validator with current state/sector knowledge has validated them, recorded by role (Appendix
A: owners require current state/sector validation).

## Relevant procedure and deadlines

- Procedure: ___ (name it; link the official text)
- Statutory or administrative deadline, if any: ___
- Prerequisites or exclusions: ___
- Design target that applies, in the pack's words (section 4): "Suggested process targets: receipt
  immediately digitally or within two working days with assistance; routing within seven calendar
  days; ordinary response within 21 calendar days unless governing law specifies otherwise; systemic
  admission decision within 30 days; hearing/decision normally within 60/90 days of admission. These
  are design targets. Applicable law and urgent safety procedures take precedence."

## Evidence

- Source: ___ (link)
- Denominator: ___ (what the figure is counted against, and the period)
- Uncertainty: ___ (what the source does not settle)

No evidence is asserted to have already been collected unless the brief says who collected it and
when.

## Counterargument

The strongest case against the proposed change, in the words of someone who holds it. ___

## Proposed change

What should change, who would do it, and what lawful power lets them. ___

Government can reject a preferred policy with defensible reasons. Write the change so a defensible
refusal is possible and recognisable.

## Safe challenge route

Routes from the card (A, I, R, H, J, P, V) and which one applies first here, with its limits: ___

Routes are options, not a compulsory ladder. Urgent protection and individual relief must not wait
for a public hearing. Filing here must not make people miss statutory appeal or litigation
deadlines.

## Notes on using the template

- The brief is about one issue family. If it needs two, write two.
- Keep the pack's vocabulary: issue family, challenge route, lead office, co-owner, reasoned
  response, verified transfer, preserved dissent, evidence of change.
- Do not include complainant data. A brief describes a pattern and a procedure, not a case.
- The generated site links to this template from every issue page.
