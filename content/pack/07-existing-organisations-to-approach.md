# 7. Existing organisations to approach: evidence and bounded requests

Selection basis: publicly documented relevant work and a plausible contribution to this agenda. “Remote task” is our suggested request, not an advertised opening unless explicitly noted. Willingness, capacity, affiliation with the movement and remote arrangements remain unconfirmed for every entry. Use institutional public routes; no personal activist contact database is included.

## 01. Association for Democratic Reforms / National Election Watch

Documented relevance: Electoral and political transparency; public reports on candidates, funding and expenditure.

Agenda links: 001–005. Suggested remote contribution: Review the electoral-accountability language and one public-data verification checklist.

Status: Current reports establish relevant work; a current remote volunteer opportunity is not confirmed.

Official evidence: <https://adrindia.org/?page=0>

Public institutional route: <https://www.adrindia.org/contact-us>

## 02. Internet Freedom Foundation (IFF)

Documented relevance: Digital rights, privacy and freedom of expression; its volunteer page includes technology and design contributions.

Agenda links: 009–010, 091–096. Suggested remote contribution: Review the privacy/retaliation model; test an accessible synthetic intake or appeal interface.

Status: Public volunteer invitation found; task acceptance and remote arrangements still need confirmation.

Official evidence: <https://internetfreedom.in/volunteer-for-digital-freedom/>

Public institutional route: <https://internetfreedom.in/contact/>

## 03. Software Freedom Law Center, India (SFLC.IN)

Documented relevance: Digital-rights legal and policy work; public volunteer invitation.

Agenda links: 091–096. Suggested remote contribution: Review surveillance, shutdown and platform-remedy descriptions; identify unsupported legal claims.

Status: Public volunteer invitation found; no commitment to this project or legal representation.

Official evidence: <https://sflc.in/volunteer/>

Public institutional route: <https://sflc.in/>

## 04. Satark Nagrik Sangathan (SNS)

Documented relevance: RTI, government accountability, public entitlements, complaints and public hearings.

Agenda links: 004–008, 037, 067, 088. Suggested remote contribution: Stress-test the reasoned-response and refusal pathways using a consented anonymised case.

Status: Mandate supports fit; remote availability not confirmed.

Official evidence: <https://www.snsindia.org/about/>

Public institutional route: <https://www.snsindia.org/>

## 05. Mazdoor Kisan Shakti Sangathan (MKSS)

Documented relevance: Workers’ rights, transparency and community verification through public hearings.

Agenda links: 004–006, 022, 071–080. Suggested remote contribution: Review the public-hearing design so documents and software do not displace community testimony.

Status: Grassroots movement with relevant practice; no current remote invitation verified.

Official evidence: <https://mkssindia.org/pages/aboutus.html>

Public institutional route: <https://mkssindia.org/>

## 06. People’s Union for Civil Liberties (PUCL)

Documented relevance: Civil liberties and human-rights work; membership, internships and campaign participation described publicly.

Agenda links: 009, 011–020, 051–060. Suggested remote contribution: Review due process, protected testimony and retaliation safeguards with the appropriate branch.

Status: Public participation routes found; branch capacity and remote work require confirmation.

Official evidence: <https://pucl.org/about-us/>

Public institutional route: <https://pucl.org/>

## 07. LibTech India

Documented relevance: Action research connecting public records, technology and public-service accountability.

Agenda links: 023, 067, 080, 092, 099. Suggested remote contribution: Audit one welfare-service journey and build a privacy-safe evidence template for exclusions.

Status: Research fit verified; no current remote volunteer opening confirmed.

Official evidence: <https://libtech.in/>

Public institutional route: <https://libtech.in/contact/>

## 08. Centre for Budget and Governance Accountability / People’s Budget Initiative

Documented relevance: Public budgets, accountable governance and civil-society participation in budget priorities.

Agenda links: 006, 031–050, 088, 098. Suggested remote contribution: Review the budget-change pathway and a public-spending evidence checklist.

Status: Think-tank/network fit verified; participation and remote review capacity unconfirmed.

Official evidence: <https://www.cbgaindia.org/peoples-budget-initiative-pbi/>

Public institutional route: <https://www.cbgaindia.org/>

## 09. Centre for Internet and Society (CIS)

Documented relevance: Research on digital technology, privacy, expression, accessibility and openness.

Agenda links: 008–010, 057, 091–096. Suggested remote contribution: Critique identity separation, AI-assisted routing and open standards in the proposed software.

Status: Research/collaboration relevance found; do not assume movement endorsement or volunteer placement.

Official evidence: <https://cis-india.org/about>

Public institutional route: <https://cis-india.org/>

## 10. Commonwealth Human Rights Initiative (CHRI), India

Documented relevance: Access to information and access to justice, including police reform.

Agenda links: 008, 011–015. Suggested remote contribution: Review police/prison complaint routes and the limits of rights-body powers.

Status: Institutional work supports fit; present capacity and remote arrangements need confirmation.

Official evidence: <https://humanrightsinitiative.org.in/>

Public institutional route: <https://humanrightsinitiative.org.in/>

## 11. National Centre for Promotion of Employment for Disabled People (NCPEDP)

Documented relevance: Disability inclusion; institutional site currently states it is being revamped.

Agenda links: 002, 040, 043, 057, 061–070. Suggested remote contribution: Request a disability-led review of voting/intake accessibility and reasonable accommodation.

Status: Contact route verified; detailed current programme/remote availability needs reconfirmation.

Official evidence: <https://ncpedp.org/>

Public institutional route: <https://ncpedp.org/>

## 12. Parisar, Pune

Documented relevance: Sustainable urban development, transport, air quality and road safety.

Agenda links: 068–070, 081, 090. Suggested remote contribution: Review a Pune transport or air-quality issue brief and an evidence-based public-hearing format.

Status: Pune civic relevance verified; remote contribution is a proposed request.

Official evidence: <https://parisar.org/who-we-are/about-us>

Public institutional route: <https://parisar.org/>

## 13. Janwani, Pune

Documented relevance: Sustainable development and waste-management collaboration; website invites volunteers.

Agenda links: 065, 070, 088. Suggested remote contribution: Review a local waste-service accountability case and community communication materials.

Status: Public volunteer invitation found; role, politics and remote arrangements must be agreed separately.

Official evidence: <https://www.janwanipune.org/about-us>

Public institutional route: <https://www.janwanipune.org/>

Additional network lead: SNS’s official page describes collaboration with NCPRI, the Right to Food campaign, Campaign for Judicial Accountability and Reform and Pension Parishad. These are possible referral routes, not verified participants in the unnamed movement. The NCPRI website could not be retrieved in this check, so a current direct contact is not supplied.
